### Two counters went up, and that is the intended result

*Distinct* duplicated paragraphs rose 869 → 1,193 and distinct duplicated
headings rose 321 → 511. This is what breaking up boilerplate looks like:
one heading that appeared on 371 pages becomes eight that appear on ~46 each.
More distinct strings are technically "duplicated", far fewer pages carry any
given one. The counter that reflects reader experience is **instances**, and
that fell.

## Layout and rendering

58 page/viewport combinations measured with CDP at 1440×1000 and 390×844
(home, contact, quote, 4 state pages, 4 city pages, 4 categories, 8 products
across different categories, 3 resources, 3 blogs):

**0 issues.** No horizontal document overflow, exactly one footer, exactly one
H1, at most one FAQ block, no empty tall sections, no vertical gap above 180px,
no heading breaking mid-word, on every page at both widths.

### Fixed

- **Section padding was resolving to its ceiling.** `--section-y` was
  `clamp(3.75rem, 2.5rem + 5vw, 7rem)`, which at 1440px hit 7rem and gave every
  section 224px of vertical padding around 330–550px of content. Fourteen
  sections of that put roughly a third of the page height into padding. Ceiling
  reduced; floor untouched so mobile rhythm is unchanged.
  California desktop 8,917px → 8,301px, Texas 8,682px → 8,066px, both mobile
  heights unchanged.
- **`.sec-link`** was an undefined class in the CSS audit; now styled.

### Reported defects that did not reproduce

Checked directly rather than taken on trust:

- **Duplicate FAQ blocks / duplicate footers** — 0 on every page. Never present.
- **Table overflow on mobile** — `table.data` has `min-width: 480px` and looks
  like a 390px overflow, but every instance is wrapped in `.table-scroll`
  (`overflow-x: auto`). `scrollWidth` equals `clientWidth` on every page tested.
- **Blank white card images** — a headless lazy-loading artifact, not missing
  files. All 1,947 renditions exist and serve 200 with correct content-type.
- **"Random black decorative shapes"** — the only off-canvas elements are two
  `.hp` spam honeypots (`position:absolute; left:-9999px; aria-hidden`).
- **Extremely narrow, tall quote form** — the location quote form measures
  within its column at both widths; no sticky container creating blank space.

### Not changed, and why

The location prose sections centre a ~630px text column inside a 1,580px wrap,
leaving wide empty flanks. This is the design's deliberate editorial measure,
and `--wrap: 1580px` was an explicit client decision. Reducing the vertical
padding addressed the height complaint; restructuring the 10 authored location
layout variants late in the session was not a call to make unilaterally.
**Flagging it as a design decision worth revisiting, not a bug.**

## What changed, by area

### Products (371)

- **43 fact records written** this session (Electronics 9, Jewellery 7,
  Sustainable 6, Promotional 5, CBD 4, Home Fragrance 3, Containers 3, Pharma 2,
  Wellness 2, Bands 2), completing coverage at **371/371**. Validated: no
  missing or extra slugs, no duplicate keys, all 117 `pairs` references resolve.
- **Fixed band headings now derive from traits.** "Get a price" ×371, "Why
  buyers specify this format" ×371, "Often specified alongside this" ×371 and
  the four benefit-tile labels ×371 were template constants. `product-headings.ts`
  derives them from the same traits that already choose the page layout, seeded
  on the slug so a page keeps its heading between builds.
- **Universal FAQ answers keep their factual core word-for-word.** The 100-unit
  minimum, the 3–5 day window, the $0.30 floor, the file requirements and the
  proofing sequence are unchanged — rewording those per page is the kind of
  uniqueness the client explicitly ruled out. Each answer instead gains one
  sentence true only of that product: what to check on its sample, what makes
  its freight awkward, which part of its artwork prepress actually looks at.
- **Related products no longer identical within a category.** The score tiebreak
  was alphabetical, so every product in a category surfaced the same four
  siblings — the cause of 35 groups of pages sharing an identical outbound link
  set. Authored `pairs` now lead; the tiebreak is seeded on both slugs.

### Service / info pages (12)

- Each section now carries **one** contextual internal link, chosen for the
  question that section raises (33 sections). Previously all twelve pages shared
  the same two links, which is what put them in an identical-link-set group.
- Closing bands (quote heading, lede, FAQ heading) written per page. Previously
  four identical headings on all twelve.

### Locations (40), categories (26), resources (15), blogs (10)

Already rewritten before this session, and measured clean: **0 pairs above 20%
similarity** in all four groups. Not touched, except that resources were
expanded and given their full 10 FAQs after the gap above was found.

   45–50%. Pushing past that means rewording facts, which the client vetoed.

   filler, which the brief prohibits.
3. **13 undefined CSS classes and 2 undefined vars** predate this work and are
   worth a cleanup pass.
