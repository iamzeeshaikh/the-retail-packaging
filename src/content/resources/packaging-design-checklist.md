This checklist is for the design stage specifically — the decisions made between receiving a dieline and releasing artwork to production.

## Before opening a design file

- Dieline received from the supplier for the confirmed size
- Panel orientation understood: which face is front, which is hidden under the glue lap
- Regulated content collected at final length, not as placeholder text
- Substrate confirmed, since kraft and uncoated stocks change colour behaviour
- Print method confirmed, since flexo and offset have different detail limits
- Finish decisions made, since spot UV and emboss need artwork designed for them

## Hierarchy and legibility

- One clear primary message readable from three feet
- Brand mark positioned on the panel that faces the shopper on shelf
- Variant or flavour distinguishable at a glance from neighbouring SKUs
- Product name legible at the smallest size the pack will appear
- Secondary information genuinely secondary, not competing

## Regulated panel

- Ingredient, INCI or supplement facts panel laid out at legal minimum size or larger
- Allergen declarations present and prominent where required
- Net weight, batch space and country of origin included
- State-specific warnings confirmed for every market
- Compliance sign-off obtained in writing before release

## Structure interaction

- No text or barcode crossing a fold line
- Critical content held inside safety margins on every panel
- Nothing important positioned under a glue lap
- Interior print considered where the pack opens to reveal it
- Window position checked against the product inside

## Print constraints

- Colour mode CMYK, spot colours named consistently
- Rich black on large solids, 100% K on small text
- Positive text above 6pt, reversed text above 8pt
- Fine detail simplified where foil or emboss is applied
- Gradients checked if printing flexo on corrugated
- Total ink coverage within press limit

## Barcode

- Placed on a flat panel, not across a fold or curve
- At or above 80% of nominal size
- Dark bars on a light background with a clear quiet zone
- Scan-tested on the printed substrate, not from a screen

## Pre-flight before release

- Images 300 dpi at placed size
- Fonts outlined or embedded
- Bleed on every trim edge
- Dieline layer separate and non-printing
- Unused swatches removed
- File named with product, size and version

## After the proof

- Proof checked against a written specification, not memory
- Physical sample reviewed where fit or colour is critical
- Approval given by someone authorised to give it
- Approved file archived with dieline, board spec and colour targets
