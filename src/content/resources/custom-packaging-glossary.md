Packaging quotes use vocabulary that is rarely explained. These are the terms that appear most often, defined the way they are actually used.

## Structure and materials

**Caliper** — board thickness, quoted in points (thousandths of an inch) or millimetres.

**Flute** — the corrugated wave between liners. Letter grades (F, E, B, C, BC) denote thickness and strength.

**SBS** — solid bleached sulphate. White coated virgin paperboard with the best print surface.

**CCNB** — clay-coated news back. Recycled paperboard with a coated printing surface and grey reverse.

**Greyboard** — thick rigid board used for non-collapsing boxes, wrapped in printed paper.

**Kraft** — unbleached paper or board. Brown, absorbs moisture, shifts printed colour warmer.

**Moulded pulp** — recycled paper slurry pressed into a shape, used for protective components.

**Mono-material** — packaging built from a single polymer family so it can be recycled as one stream.

## Design and prepress

**Dieline** — the flat vector template showing cut, fold and glue positions.

**Bleed** — artwork extended past the trim edge so cutting variance never exposes bare board.

**Safety margin** — the distance critical content is held inside trim and fold lines.

**Score** — a pressed crease that lets board fold cleanly without cracking.

**Trapping** — slight overlap between adjacent colours to hide registration variance.

**Rich black** — a black built from multiple inks rather than 100% K alone, used on large solids.

**Registration** — the alignment of separate colour plates or finishing passes.

## Printing

**Offset lithography** — plate-based printing via a rubber blanket. Best colour accuracy on flat board.

**Flexography** — plate-based printing used on corrugated, film and bags.

**Rotogravure** — engraved cylinder printing for very high volume flexible film.

**Litho-lamination** — an offset-printed sheet bonded to corrugated for photographic quality.

**Makeready** — press setup before a run begins. A fixed cost regardless of quantity.

**Spot colour** — a named premixed ink rather than a four-colour build.

## Finishing

**Lamination** — a plastic film bonded to the printed surface for durability.

**Aqueous coating** — water-based inline coating that protects without breaking recyclability.

**Spot UV** — high-gloss varnish on selected areas, effective only against a matte background.

**Foil stamping** — metallic foil applied by heated die.

**Emboss / deboss** — raised or recessed relief pressed into the board.

## Commercial and logistics

**MOQ** — minimum order quantity, set by format and print method rather than policy.

**Tooling** — the cutting die or stamping die made for your specification. A one-time cost reused on reorders.

**Price break** — a quantity threshold where unit price steps down.

**Dimensional weight** — the volumetric weight carriers charge on when it exceeds actual weight.

**Retail-ready packaging** — a case that opens into a shelf display without unpacking.

**Knocked down flat** — supplied unassembled to reduce freight volume.

Terms that appear on your own quote and are not listed here are worth asking about. A specification you cannot read is a specification you cannot compare, and our [breakdown of what drives packaging pricing](/resources/packaging-cost-factors/) explains how these terms translate into cost.
